Title: Law Enforcement Requests

Draft for review. This page is a plain, customer-protective draft and is not legal advice; the final wording is pending Chris's lawyer.

1. How to make a request. Law enforcement requests for user data must be in writing, on official letterhead, and sent to the contact listed here. We review each request for legal sufficiency.

2. What we require. We require valid legal process appropriate to the data sought (for example, a subpoena, court order, or warrant as the law requires). We do not hand over data on an informal request.

3. Notice to users. Unless we are legally prohibited, or there is a risk to someone's safety, we try to tell the affected user before we disclose their data, so they can respond.

4. Emergencies. In a genuine emergency involving a risk of death or serious harm, we may disclose limited data to the extent the law allows.

5. Contact. Send requests to the address in the footer, marked "Law Enforcement".
