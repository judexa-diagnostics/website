Title: Copyright and Abuse

Draft for review. This page is a plain, customer-protective draft and is not legal advice; the final wording is pending Chris's lawyer. The designated copyright agent is registered once the lawyer approves.

1. Reporting copyright infringement. If you believe content on InPhox infringes your copyright, send a written notice to our designated agent with: your signature; identification of the work; identification of the material and where it is; your contact details; a statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised; and a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the notice is accurate and you are authorised to act.

2. Counter-notice. If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake, you may send a counter-notice with the same kinds of detail.

3. Repeat infringers. Accounts that repeatedly infringe are suspended or closed.

4. Other abuse. To report fraud, harassment, or other abuse of the Service, contact support with the details and we will review it.

5. Designated agent. The registered copyright agent's name and address will be listed here once registered. Until then, send notices to support and mark them "Copyright".
