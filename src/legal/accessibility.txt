Title: Accessibility

Draft for review. This is a plain statement of intent, not a certification.

1. Our aim. We want InPhox to be usable by everyone, and we work toward the WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines: readable text, keyboard navigation, a skip link to the main content, meaningful labels, and colour that is not the only way meaning is shown.

2. Known gaps. Some marketing animations are decorative and can be skipped; if any content is hard to use, tell us and we will fix it.

3. How to reach us. If you hit an accessibility barrier, contact us (details in the footer) and we will respond and work with you on an alternative.
