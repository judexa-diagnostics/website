Title: Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use InPhox, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification or other assistive technology.

1. Our standard. We work toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. That includes readable text sizes, full keyboard use, a skip link to the main content, labels on every control, and colour that is never the only way meaning is shown.

2. Motion. Decorative animations follow your device's reduce-motion setting and can be turned off on the website.

3. Known gaps. Some interactive demos on the website are visual walkthroughs; the same information is in the text around them. If anything is hard to use, tell us and we will fix it or give you the information another way.

4. Contact. Email support@inphox.net with the page and what got in the way. We aim to answer within two business days.
