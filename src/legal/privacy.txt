Title: Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information InPhox LLC ("InPhox", "we", "us") collects when you use InPhox (the "Service"), how we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and your choices. It is part of our Terms of Service.

Our short version. We collect what we need to run, secure and improve the Service and keep it only as long as we need it. We do not sell individuals' names, contact details, government ID or ID-document data, and we do not share personal information for advertising. We may share and sell business data, such as pricing, inventory and device information, and de-identified or aggregated data.

What you give us. Your name, public handle, email address, mobile number, shipping address, profile picture link and how you sign in. If you run a shop: your business details, the people you invite and the records you keep in InPhox (inventory, customers, sales, purchases, invoices and check history). Messages you send to support.

Device checks. When you check a device we process the identifiers you enter or scan (such as IMEI or serial number) and the results that come back, and we keep the check history for your account. For Activation Lock we only show whether a lock is reported as on; we never read, store or get around anyone's Apple account or lock.

Payments. Card payments are handled by our payment processor. We never see or store your full card number or security code. We keep the card brand, last four digits, expiry month and year and your billing history.

What we collect automatically. The devices and browsers you sign in on, your sessions, a security log of sign-ins and account changes, and basic technical data (internet address, device type, app version) needed to keep the Service secure. We use only the cookies needed to sign you in and keep your account safe.

How we use it. To provide the Service and the features you ask for; to verify your email, phone and address; to bill you; to prevent fraud, abuse, scraping and security incidents; to provide support; to send you service messages; and to meet our legal duties.

What sellers see. A seller you deal with sees your public handle and that you are verified. They see your name, phone and address only for an order with them.

Sellers' customer data. When a shop records its own customers in InPhox, the shop decides what it collects and is responsible for it; we process that data on the shop's behalf. Customers of a shop should ask the shop about their records.

What we share and sell. Service providers that run parts of the Service for us (hosting, payments, email and text delivery, address checking, device data sources), only for that work and under contracts that bind them to protect it; sellers, as described above; authorities or others when the law requires it or to protect the rights, property or safety of InPhox, our users or the public; and a buyer or successor if InPhox is merged or sold. We may also share and sell business, pricing, inventory, device and usage information and de-identified or aggregated data with other companies. We do not sell individuals' names, contact details, government ID or ID-document data.

Staff access. InPhox support staff can see your account to help you, to fix a problem or to stop fraud. Every look is logged.

How long we keep it. Sign-in links and one-time codes: removed 7 days after they can no longer be used. Records of finished email or phone changes: 30 days. Ended sessions: about 13 months (400 days). The security log and your agreement record: for the life of your account and as long afterwards as security, disputes and legal duties need. Earlier phones and addresses: while orders that used them need them. Billing and tax records: as long as the law requires, usually up to 7 years. When you delete your account we delete or de-identify the rest within 30 days, except what we must keep for those reasons.

Security. We use encryption in transit, hashed credentials, access logging and least-privilege access. No system is perfectly secure.

Your choices and rights. You can see and change your details in My InPhox, download what we keep about you from Data export, and delete your account. Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have the right to know what we collect, to get a copy, to correct or delete it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. Email support@inphox.net from your account email; we verify the request, answer within the time the law allows and never treat you differently for using your rights.

Children. The Service is not for anyone under 16. If you think a child gave us information, tell us and we will delete it.

Where data is kept. We store and process information in the United States. Using the Service from elsewhere means you agree to that.

When you agree. We record the version you agreed to, the time and a scrambled (hashed) form of your internet address, not the address itself.

Changes. We may change this policy. When we do, we change the Version line above and ask you to agree again the next time you sign in.

Contact: InPhox LLC, support@inphox.net
