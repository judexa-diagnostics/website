Title: Privacy Policy
Status: Draft, needs Chris's lawyer

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information InPhox LLC ("InPhox", "we", "us") collects when you use InPhox (the "Service"), how we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and your choices. It is part of our Terms of Service.

Our short version. We collect what we need to run, secure and improve the Service and keep it only as long as we need it. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. We may share or sell business data that identifies no person, such as prices, stock levels and device models, and de-identified or aggregated data.

Notice at collection. When you sign up we collect your name, email address and how you sign in. When you verify your account or open a shop we also collect your mobile number, address and business details. We use them to run and secure your account and the Service as this policy describes, keep them as long as "How long we keep it" says, and never sell or share them. If you use the diagnostics app's location test, it also collects the phone's precise location, which California law treats as sensitive personal information; we use it only to run that test and show its result, never to infer anything about you.

What you give us. Your name, public handle, email address, mobile number, shipping address, profile picture link and how you sign in. If you run a shop: your business details, the people you invite and the records you keep in InPhox (inventory, customers, sales, purchases, invoices and check history). Messages you send to support. If you write to us through the website's contact form: your name, email address, company and message, which we use to answer you.

Device checks. When you check a device we process the identifiers you enter or scan (such as IMEI or serial number) and the results that come back, and we keep the check history for your account. For Activation Lock we only show whether a lock is reported as on; we never read, store or get around anyone's Apple account or lock.

Payments. Card payments are handled by our payment processor. We never see or store your full card number or security code. We keep the card brand, last four digits, expiry month and year and your billing history.

What we collect automatically. The devices and browsers you sign in on, your sessions, a security log of sign-ins and account changes, and basic technical data (internet address, device type, app version) needed to keep the Service secure. We use only the cookies needed to sign you in and keep your account safe.

Categories, sources and purposes. In the last 12 months we collected these categories of personal information: identifiers (name, handle, email address, mobile number, internet address and device identifiers); customer and commercial records (addresses, billing history, card brand and last four digits, orders and check history); internet and app activity (sign-ins, sessions, the security log and app events); precise geolocation (only from the diagnostics app's location test); and professional information (your business details and role). We get it from you, from your devices and your use of the Service, from the shop you work for or deal with, and from our service providers (such as payment processing and address checking). We use it for the purposes in "How we use it" and disclose it only to the recipients in "What we share", for those purposes.

How we use it. To provide the Service and the features you ask for; to verify your email, phone and address; to bill you; to prevent fraud, abuse, scraping and security incidents; to provide support; to send you service messages; and to meet our legal duties.

What sellers see. A seller you deal with sees your public handle and that you are verified. They see your name, phone and address only for an order with them.

Sellers' customer data. When a shop records its own customers in InPhox, the shop decides what it collects and is responsible for it. We process that data only on the shop's behalf and under the data processing terms in section 20 of our Terms of Service: we do not sell it, share it or use it to build our own products. Customers of a shop should ask the shop about their records; requests that reach us go to the shop.

Diagnostics app. When a phone is tested with the InPhox diagnostics app, the app reads the phone's model, identifiers (such as IMEI or serial number) and the result of each test, and keeps them with the check history of the shop that ran it. The location test reads the phone's location, shows it on a map and keeps the coordinates with that test's result. To draw the map, the phone loads map pictures of that area from a public map service, which sees the area and the phone's internet address.

Desktop station. The InPhox desktop station sends usage events to its shop's InPhox, such as that the station started, a phone was connected or a test began, finished or failed, with the station's name and the email of the staff member signed in. We use them to keep the station working and to improve it.

What we share. Service providers that run parts of the Service for us (hosting, payments, email and text delivery, address checking, device data sources), only for that work and under contracts that bind them to protect it; sellers, as described above; authorities or others when the law requires it or to protect the rights, property or safety of InPhox, our users or the public; and a buyer or successor if InPhox is merged or sold. We do not sell or share personal information, as California law uses those words, and we do not use it for advertising. We may share or sell business data that identifies no person, such as prices, stock levels and device models, and de-identified or aggregated data, which we keep de-identified, never try to tie back to a person, and require anyone who receives it not to tie back either.

Staff access. InPhox support staff can see your account to help you, to fix a problem or to stop fraud. Every look is logged.

How long we keep it. Sign-in links and one-time codes: removed 7 days after they can no longer be used. Records of finished email or phone changes: 30 days. Ended sessions: about 13 months (400 days). The security log and your agreement record: for the life of your account and as long afterwards as security, disputes and legal duties need. Earlier phones and addresses: while orders that used them need them. Billing and tax records: as long as the law requires, usually up to 7 years. When you delete your account we delete or de-identify the rest within 30 days, except what we must keep for those reasons.

Security. We use encryption in transit, hashed credentials, access logging and least-privilege access. No system is perfectly secure.

Your choices and rights. You can see and change your details in My InPhox, download what we keep about you from Data export, and delete your account. Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have the right to know what we collect, to get a copy, to correct or delete it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of; if that ever changed, we would add a way to opt out first and honour your browser's opt-out signal. Email support@inphox.net from your account email; we verify the request, answer within the time the law allows and never treat you differently for using your rights. You may use an authorised agent with your signed permission; we may still ask you to confirm your identity directly. If we decline a request we tell you why, and you can ask us to reconsider by replying; if we still decline, you can contact your state attorney general.

Do Not Track. We do not track you across other websites, and no other company collects information about your activity over time or across websites through InPhox. So a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal changes nothing: there is no tracking to turn off. If that ever changed, we would honour those signals as an opt-out.

People in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. The Service is offered to businesses in the United States. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, InPhox LLC is the controller of your account information; for a shop's records about its own customers, the shop is the controller and we process them for it. Our legal bases are: our contract with you (your account and the features you use); our legitimate interests in securing the Service, preventing fraud, abuse and scraping, and improving the Service, which we weigh against your rights; legal duties (such as billing, tax and lawful requests); and your consent where we ask for it, which you can withdraw at any time. You have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to restrict or object to how we use it, to get a copy in a portable form (Data export), and to complain to your local data protection authority. If you object to a use based on our legitimate interests, we stop unless we have compelling grounds or need it for legal claims.

Children. The Service is only for people 18 or older, the same age as our Terms of Service. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think someone under 18 gave us information, tell us and we will delete it.

Where data is kept. We store and process information in the United States. If you use the Service from outside the United States, you give your information to us directly in the United States. Where our service providers receive personal information from outside the United States, we use the safeguards the law requires, such as standard contractual clauses.

When you agree. We record the version you agreed to, the time and a scrambled (hashed) form of your internet address, not the address itself.

Changes. We may change this policy. Each version takes effect on the date in its Version line. When we change it, we change the Version line above, tell you about important changes by email or in the Service, and ask you to agree again the next time you sign in.

Contact: InPhox LLC, support@inphox.net
