Title: Subprocessors

InPhox uses a small number of service providers ("subprocessors") to run the Service. Each one may handle personal information only to do its work for us, and only under a written contract that requires it to protect that information to the standard in our Terms of Service (section 20) and Privacy Policy. We stay responsible for their work.

1. Hosting and databases. Cloud hosting and database providers in the United States that store and serve the Service and its backups.

2. Payments. A card payment processor that handles subscription charges, card checks and Marketplace payments. We never see or store your full card number.

3. Email and text messages. Providers that deliver sign-in links, one-time codes, receipts and service messages.

4. Shipping. Carrier and label providers, used only when you buy or print a shipping label. They receive the names and addresses on that shipment.

5. Address checking. Address data sources used to check and complete addresses as you type them.

6. Device data sources. Carrier, manufacturer and industry databases used for device checks. They receive the device identifier (such as IMEI or serial number), never your name or your customers' names.

7. Maps. A public map service that supplies the map pictures in the diagnostics app's location test. It sees the map area and the phone's internet address.

8. Changes. We give shops at least 30 days' notice, by email or in the Service, before a new subprocessor starts handling their customers' personal information. A shop can object by emailing support@inphox.net within that time; if we cannot reasonably address the objection, the shop may stop using the affected feature or close its account. Shops can ask for the current list of providers by emailing support@inphox.net.
