Title: Subprocessors

Draft for review. This page is a plain, customer-protective draft and is not legal advice; the final wording is pending Chris's lawyer. It lists the categories of third parties that help run the Service; the specific vendor names are confirmed with the lawyer before publishing.

1. Hosting and infrastructure. Cloud hosting and database providers that store and serve the Service.

2. Payments. Payment processors that handle card and payout transactions; InPhox does not store full card numbers.

3. Email and notifications. Services that deliver transactional email and push notifications.

4. Shipping. Carrier and label providers used when you buy a shipping label.

5. Maps and device checks. On-device and network services used by the diagnostics and scanning features.

6. Updates. When this list changes, we update this page. Contact support to ask which specific vendors are in use.
