What it does

Double Puff runs on a Windows PC or a Mac at your bench. Plug a phone into it while a lot is open on the Intake website and it:

  • reads the model, storage, colour, IMEI, serial, battery, parts and locks off the phone, for free (no check is used);
  • listens for Face ID or Touch ID (Biometrics);
  • sets an iPhone at the Hello screen up for you, if your shop turned that on (Auto activate);
  • installs the inphox diagnostic app, runs the tests and removes the app again (Android, iPhone);
  • erases phones with a signed certificate (Secure erase).

Double Puff is on Growth and up.

Two screens

You work on two screens side by side: Double Puff on the computer the phones plug into, and the Intake website in Chrome or Edge where the lot is open. Phones only go into a lot while that lot's screen is open, signed in as the same person as the station (Keep the lot open).

Help inside the app

Press ? at the top of Double Puff for short step-by-step replays: plugging in, trusting the computer, Wi-Fi, a phone that's stuck, the diagnostic app. Each one has Read more, which opens the matching page here, and Report an issue.

When a phone needs you, its card says what to do next in two or three numbered steps. The same steps are in the replays and on these pages.

What you need

A Windows PC or a MacRuns Double Puff. On Windows, an account that can approve the admin prompt.
USB data cablesLightning or USB-C data cables; charge-only cables won't read. Plug in as many phones at once as you have ports; a powered USB hub helps (Cables and hubs).
Chrome or EdgeFor the Intake website. Other browsers can't reach the label printer.
A phone as the cameraRecommended for scanning licenses: a phone camera reads the barcode first time; a webcam often doesn't.
Label printer (optional)A small thermal label printer with 50 × 50 mm labels, by USB or Bluetooth, or a ZPL label printer. Without one, intake works the same; you just don't get stickers.
Tablet (optional)For Customer View, where the customer watches the prices and signs.
