Sign in

  1. Open your shop's address, or home.inphox.net. The shop sends you to the inphox sign-in page by itself.
  2. Continue with Google, or Continue with Apple, Sign in with a passkey, or your inphox email and password. Use the email your owner invited.
  3. You're in. You land back in your shop, or on Home. Next time it remembers you.

Several stores

One account opens every store you work at. Home lists them; tap one to open it.

No account yet

Create one on the sign-in page with the email your owner invited, then open the store again.

Signed in from before

If a page says You can't open this screen, or Employees says it can't check your seats, sign out once and sign in again with Continue. That links your old login to your inphox account.

At the station

Double Puff signs in with the same account: it opens the sign-in page in your browser. See Sign in at the station.

Sign out

Account menu › Sign out. Signing out clears what the app kept on that device.
