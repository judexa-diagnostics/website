The tabs

Home
Your stores with today's numbers, To do (what needs you now), and what you bought from other shops. With several stores, pick one at the top or swipe between them.
Orders
Orders across your stores. Tap one to open it in its shop.
CRM
Leads and customers across your stores, on plans that include CRM (Growth and up).
Marketplace
Buy and sell between shops. Until it opens for your area it says The Marketplace isn't open yet; everything else works as usual.

The account menu

Your profile, Personal details, Security (where you're signed in), Verify (email, phone, address), Locations, Stores you buy from, Billing (your plan, Change plan, Add-ons, cards and invoices) and Help & downloads (support's email, the phone app and Double Puff).

A store's page

Open a store from Home: Overview, Issues, Customers and Employees.

After you open a new store

When your store is built you see a short celebration, then Home opens on your new store.
