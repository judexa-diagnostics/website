Before you start

  • Keep the phones plugged into Double Puff: the station does the erasing, and only while the phone is connected with the app running.
  • Every owner must have signed out of Find My (Apple) or removed their Google account. An erase never removes Activation Lock or a Google account lock.
  • Secure erasure is on Growth and up.

From a lot

  1. Tap the number at the top left of a phone's card. It turns into a tick and the card gets a red outline; the top bar becomes the erase bar. (Or press Secure erase in the top bar first.)
  2. Tap other cards to add them, tap again to drop one. Select all takes every phone the checks clear.
  3. Press Erase N, tick Every owner signed out of Find My, then press Erase. That tick is your approval.
  4. Watch the card: Erasing 40%, then Erased · Certificate. It never holds up the lot.

From Station phones (no lot)

  1. Intake website › Station phones. Tick the phones, or Select all. Phones with Find My on are left out automatically, with a note.
  2. Erase, then pick Erase (leaves the phone at the Hello screen) or Erase and activate.
  3. Tick the owner signed out of Find My › Erase.
  4. Each card shows a progress bar, the step and about how long is left. Retry after a failure, Cancel to stop.

Erase and activate

Activates the phone after the erase and checks that nothing manages it, so it's ready for a new owner. It joins the Phone Wi-Fi to do that.

The certificate

Signed, with the MDM and Find My answers (On or Off). Not checked only when nothing was checked. Your logo goes on it (Settings › General).

Unplugged mid-erase

An erase restarts the phone. If it drops off before its check finished, the lot waits 10 minutes for it and shows a countdown; Add 5 minutes, up to 30 in all. Plug it back in and it carries on.

The number won't tick

Already erased, erasing now, or not plugged into a station that can erase. Station phones says exactly why for each phone. Can't erase on this station yet means that computer's Double Puff is too old: update it.

Find My was on at intake

The phone stays blocked from erasing, even if the owner signs out later: the check from intake is what counts. Erase those by hand after the owner removes the phone from their Apple ID.

Holding period and limits

Intake › Settings › Integrations › Station phones: Holding period before a bought phone can be erased (days) (0 = no wait; useful if you hold phones in case of a police check) and Erases an hour, at most, per person and per station (0 = no limit). Hitting a limit is logged for the owner.
