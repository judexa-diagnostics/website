What counts

One phone, one checkWhen a phone comes into a lot, its first IMEI check (carrier, Find My, blacklist) and its first MDM lookup count as one check together, in either order.
Counts againAny check run again: Run Check a second time, Check MDM now after MDM already answered, a check from your inventory screen, or the same phone in another lot.
Never countsSeeing a result you already have: re-plugging a phone, opening it again, or removing it and plugging it back into the same lot (A phone stuck or half read). The station reading a phone over the cable, grading, labels, erasing, selling.
ResetsOn your billing date each month.

See what you've used

The same live bar in three places: the Intake website's sidebar (above Log out), the top of New Intake and each lot, and your shop's top bar next to your name. Each bar moves the moment a check is counted. Click it for Billing & usage: used and available checks, and extras this month.

Past your included checks

You keep working. Each extra check is 50¢, added to your bill, up to the spending limit you set. Extras are billed early, as soon as they reach half your monthly price, so a bill never piles up.

When you reach the limit

The bar turns red: Check limit reached: new phones can't be added. New lots can't start and new phones can't be added; phones already in a lot can be finished: grade, label, erase, add the customer, sign and sync as usual.

It happens at your spending limit, after 7 days of an unpaid bill, or when the shop is paused. Back to work: raise the limit or move up a plan, pay the bill, or wait for your billing date.

Safety limits

Parts checks have no limit. IMEI checks have a very high per-minute and per-hour limit per shop, and each phone a few an hour, to stop runaway checks. A normal bench never reaches them.

Shops inphox runs for free

A shop given inphox for free (not the Free plan) has no bill: the bar says Unlimited.
