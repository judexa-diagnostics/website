Where

Once, by the manager: Intake website › Settings › Integrations › Station phones › When a phone is plugged in at the Hello screen.

The three choices

Set it upThe phone is activated and taken straight to the home screen, ready to test, with no taps: language and region are set for it, and it joins the Phone Wi-Fi. MDM is then read off the phone for free. Until it's erased, the phone shows inphox as its organization.
Activate onlyThe phone is activated but stays on the setup screens. Use it when you only want to know about Activation Lock or company management.
Do nothingPhones at the Hello screen are left alone. Select one on Station phones and press Activate by hand.

What you see

The phone's card says Setting up this iPhone: leave it plugged in, no taps needed. It reaches the home screen in about a minute and the card fills in.

Already set up

A phone past the Hello screen is never touched; the station just reads it.

Company phones

If the phone belongs to a company, setup stops by itself and the card says MDM. Leave it at that screen; don't finish its setup (MDM and locks).

Activation Lock

Apple refuses to activate a locked phone. It's marked Find My on and can't be erased until the owner removes it. A phone that simply isn't activated yet is never treated as locked.

Android

Auto activate is for iPhones. An Android phone at the Welcome screen is set up with the inphox agent QR code (Plug in an Android phone).
