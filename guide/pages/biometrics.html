How it works

As soon as a phone is read, its button on the station shows Waiting…: the station is listening for as long as the phone stays plugged in. Look at the phone (Face ID) or touch the Home button (Touch ID) whenever you like. Intake and the other phones carry on meanwhile.

The ✕ on Waiting… (or the Escape key) stops listening and marks it Not tested; press the button to try again. Unplugging stops it; plugging back in starts listening again.

What the button says

StationIntake websiteWhat it means
Face ID passed / Touch ID passed (green)WorksThe sensor works. A face or finger that isn't set up on the phone still counts: the hardware answered.
No unlock seen (grey)Not confirmedThe station stopped listening without seeing an unlock. Not a fail: click it and unlock within 20 seconds.
Couldn't read (grey)Couldn't readThis phone doesn't let the station read it. Not a fail.
Face ID failed (red)FailedOnly for a real fault the phone itself reports, like Face ID disabled. Click to test again.

Grey results and Not tested never block a purchase and are never shown as a problem on the card. A real failure shows a red Biometrics — Failed line under parts.

The diagnostic app's result wins

If the inphox diagnostic app ran its own Face ID or Touch ID test on the phone, its result is the one kept (iPhone, Android).

Android fingerprint

On Android the fingerprint and face unlock are tested by the inphox test app, not by the station listening over the cable.
