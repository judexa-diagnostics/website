Cables

Data cables only (Lightning or USB-C); charge-only cables won't read. Replace any cable that makes phones drop off or read slowly.

Hubs

A powered USB hub for more phones at once. An unpowered hub often can't keep several phones awake.

How many at once

Up to 16 phones are read side by side; a 17th starts as soon as one is done. One slow phone never holds up another.

Trust

Each phone asks once per computer (Trust this computer). On a new station, phones ask again.

Restarting

No schedule needed. Restart Double Puff when a phone won't read or a card is stuck (A phone stuck or half read).
