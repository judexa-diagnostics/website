Where

Advanced › Integrations › Online Payment. Tabs: Card payments and Direct payment. Turn on one or more; you can use both.

Card payments

  1. Connect card payments. It opens the card processor's sign-up. Use your own business details: the money is paid straight to your bank, never through inphox.
  2. Stopped half way? Continue sign-up, then Check again.
  3. Ready: every invoice now has a Pay now button.

Direct payment

Write your bank or payment-app details once; every invoice shows them, and buyers leave a payment note you see on the order. You press Mark paid.

Your card details

inphox never stores card numbers: the processor keeps them.
