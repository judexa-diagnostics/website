Read off the phone, for free

For a plugged-in iPhone the station reads Find My and, after Set it up, MDM straight off the phone. That never uses a check.

The MDM lookup

Intake › Settings › Billing & usage › MDM check. It asks the manufacturer's records and catches a wiped phone a company would take back at activation. It is part of the phone's check.

OffOnly the phone's own reading. Never uses a check.
On, only when the phone can't be readEvery shop starts here. The lookup runs only when the station can't read the phone itself.
Every phoneRuns on every phone. Most thorough, uses the most checks.

Want an answer now? Check MDM now in the grading window. After MDM already answered, it counts as a new check.

A company phone at setup

Setup stops by itself and the card says MDM. Leave it at that screen; don't finish its setup.

Android: the Google account lock

An Android phone still signed in to a Google account after a reset is locked to it (FRP). Nothing at the station removes it: the owner must remove the account first.
