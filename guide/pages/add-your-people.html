Invite them

  1. Employees › Add employees. Type their email addresses, or Upload CSV for a list (up to 1,000 at a time).
  2. Send invitations. Each person gets an email. They sign in with inphox using that email and they're on your team.
  3. Set their role: Make manager or Make staff on their row. Remove takes them off the team; their work stays.

Roles

RoleWhat they can do
OwnerThe account that pays. Sees billing and can do everything.
ManagerRuns the shop: adds and removes people, shop and station settings.
StaffBuys, sells and does intake.

Seats

Each person on the team and each open invitation uses a seat. How many your plan has is on Plans. When it's full the page says Ask the owner to upgrade the plan. Double Puff stations count the same way: see People and stations.

Open invitations

Lists who hasn't joined yet: Resend or Revoke. You can't remove the owner or yourself.
