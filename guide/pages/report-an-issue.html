Email support

Email support@inphox.net . It's also under account menu › Help & downloads on home.inphox.net, and ? › Report an issue in Double Puff.

What to send

  • The reference the screen showed, like E-25B60.
  • Two screenshots: Double Puff and the Intake web page, as they look now.
  • The lot number (for example FC-12_20261006) and the last 4 of the phone's IMEI or serial.
  • The time it happened.
  • The station version from Settings › About (for example Double Puff 1.10.32 · Windows).
  • What you pressed and what you expected.

Only the last 4 digits: never send a whole IMEI, serial, password or a customer's license.

What reaches us by itself

When the Intake website or Double Puff hits an error, it is reported to inphox automatically, with the station's version. What it can't say is what you were doing, which customer, or what you expected: that part comes from you.
