  2. Scan licence: hold the back of the license in the green box. ✓ Scan Complete! fills the name, ID and address. Been here before? Returning customer.
  3. Contact Info: Cell Phone and Email. With a tablet paired, Request from Customer iPad lets the customer type them there.
  4. License Photo: Front and Back (optional).
  5. Next to Terms & Verify (Bill of sale).

Scanning a license well

  • Use a phone as the camera: it reads the barcode first time; a webcam often doesn't.
  • Hold the back of the card flat, filling the green box, in good light. Avoid glare on the barcode.
  • After you allow the camera, pick the one that focuses best from the camera list.
  • Won't scan? Type it in: no license needed to carry on.
