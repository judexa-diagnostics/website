If a payment fails

  1. The card is tried again over the next days. Everything keeps working meanwhile.
  2. After 7 days unpaid, checks pause. Everything else still works. Update the card in Billing and checks come back at once.
  3. Cancelled: the shop goes read-only. You can look at and export everything, but not buy, check or sell.
  4. 30 days later it's put in storage: switched off and kept as it was.
  5. Paid again: the shop is switched back on, exactly as you left it, and the owner gets an email.

Change plan

Account menu › Billing › Change plan. Moving up works on the next page load and gives you the extra checks for the days left; the full new price starts on your next billing date. Moving down happens on your billing date; open work finishes and old records stay readable for 30 days.

Cancel

Billing › Cancel. It ends at the end of the month you've paid for; the last extra checks are on the final bill.

Cancelled, then upgraded?

Paying for an upgrade clears a pending cancel: you keep the plan you just paid for and are never moved down afterwards.

Refund or dispute

A full refund of a plan payment puts the shop on the unpaid-bill steps. A card dispute pauses it until it's decided in the shop's favour.

Cards and invoices

Only the owner sees cards and invoices: change the card, download past invoices, or cancel.
