Stations up to date

Every station should show the same Double Puff version (Updates).

The Intake website

Chrome or Edge. Nothing to install or update. An open lot screen reconnects by itself within seconds after our updates.

Your price list

Buy prices come from your shop's price list; prices you set always win. A cell that says Ask has no automatic price. Only sizes a model really comes in are offered.

People and stations

Your plan sets how many people (Plans); Billing & usage on the Intake website shows how many stations it allows. When it's full, signing in one more says so: move up a plan, or sign out a station (or remove a person) no longer used.

Backups

Your shop (inventory, orders, customers) is backed up every night, encrypted, with a copy kept off-site. Nothing for you to do.
