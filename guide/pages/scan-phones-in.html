Add, one by one

  1. Add, the orange button in the bottom bar. Scan the IMEI, or the box barcode if it's sealed. Each scan adds a card. No light? Flashlight. Can't scan? Text input.
  2. Tap each card: pick the grade (Sealed, Open Box, A, B, C, D or DOA) and type what you paid. Suggested $X · Use fills the buy price; Use suggested buy prices does the whole cart.
  3. Save to Inventory. In stock; you're on Home.

What fills in by itself

When the check knows the phone, model, storage, colour, carrier and the Blacklist, MDM and Find My results fill in on their own, usually within about 30 seconds.

Needs a model

If a scanned phone can't be identified, its card shows a red Needs a model button. Tap it, choose the model from the list or add a new one, then grade and price as usual.

Many at once

A stack of phones or a spreadsheet: Upload inventory.

With the cable

Buying from a person at the counter, with the license and signature: use the Intake website and Double Puff (Create a lot).
