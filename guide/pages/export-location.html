Your Sub-Location

  1. Intake website › sidebar › My Settings.
  2. Your Sub-Location: pick the shelf, usually Sellable Shelf. Save.

Every phone you sync goes there (Send to your inventory). On a sellable shelf it's in stock straight away; anywhere else it's kept but not for sale until you move it.

Per person

Each person sets their own. Two people on one bench can send phones to different shelves, for example a repair bin and the sellable shelf.

Phones added in the shop

Phones you add with Add go to the shop's main stock location; the cart's Batch details shows it (Upload inventory).

Exported files

In a browser an export downloads to your downloads folder; in the phone app you pick where each file goes. Not saved means no place was picked: export again (Export).
