Open a store

  1. Start free on inphox.net, or pick a plan. Sign in or create your inphox account.
  2. Three steps: Your business, Business address, Plan and owner. The shop name keeps the capitals you type.
  3. Add a card. A credit or debit card is required, Free included; prepaid cards are not accepted. On Free a $20 hold is placed and released, and the card is never charged unless you go past your 25 included checks.
  4. Card accepted · Setting up your plan. This takes a few seconds, then your store is built.
  5. Store ready: a short celebration and Home opens on your store. You get an email too.

Didn't finish?

Sign in again and open your request: it carries on where you stopped. We keep an unfinished request for 14 days after your last change; the page says how many days are left. After that it expires: start a new one, it only takes a minute. Nothing is charged for a request that never got a card.

Paying by card or bank

Every payment can be made from a bank account; a card payment is capped at $5,000.
