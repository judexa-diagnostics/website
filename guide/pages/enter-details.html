What a phone needs

FieldHow to get it right
IMEI or serialScan it rather than type it: from Settings › General › About on an iPhone, the dialer code *#06#, or the box. One per phone; the same IMEI twice is refused. A box label or a typo is caught before it is sent.
ModelFills in from the check. Needs a model? Pick it from the list; add a new one only when it truly isn't there.
Storage and colourRead off the phone or from the check. Only sizes a model really comes in are offered. A colour you chose yourself is never overwritten.
CarrierFrom the check; Unlocked when it's free.
GradeSealed, Open Box, A, B, C, D or DOA (what each grade means).
Cost (what you paid)The buy price. Profit on every sale comes from it.
Sell priceSuggested from your cost plus your markup (10% unless you change it), never below cost. Type over it.
NotesAnything a buyer should know. Notes print on the label; red flags are added by themselves.
ShelfWhere it is. Only an Available shelf counts as in stock (Where phones can be sold).

A missing optional field never stops a step: save, and fill it in later.

Fix it later

Hold the card › Edit device (storage, colour, carrier, grade) or Adjust cost with a reason. Every open quote, order, offer, delivery and draft invoice with that phone updates by itself (One phone).

When it can't be edited

After a phone is invoiced or paid, its details are locked: issue a credit note first. Shipped or delivered phones are locked. On a confirmed order, editing asks for a short reason.
