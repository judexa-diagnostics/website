What it adds

Repair tickets, parts and outside repairers for your shop: a Repairs menu with New Repair, the Repair Board, My Repairs, Repair Visits and In Repair Now, a QC checklist on each ticket, sending a repair out to another repairer, and a log of every change. Each repair screen has a How this works expander. On Max, outside repairers can also work on your tickets.

Price

$200 a month on Basics, Growth and Max (Plans). Enterprise: in your contract.
