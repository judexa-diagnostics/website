The chips

In stock or everything, Anywhere or one location, Sort, Filter. The row stays pinned while you scroll.

Filter

  • Status: Any status, In stock, Available, Awaiting Shipment, On quote, Sold / Packing, Storage, On Hold, Repair / QC, In Transit, Customer Owned.
  • Grade, storage, carrier.
  • Checks: Blacklisted, MDM on, Account lock on, SIM locked, No locks, No buy price, Has notes.
  • How long it has sat: 30+ days, 60+ days, 90+ days.
  • Sell price: Under $100, $100 – $300, $300 – $600, $600 and up.

Ticked options move to the top. Clear wipes them, Done closes.
