Read the card

Name and IMEIModel on top, IMEI or serial under it with the last four in bold: that is what you read out.
PillsGrade, storage, colour, carrier. Green is a clean check, red a problem, yellow a carrier lock, grey nothing to worry about.
DaysHow long it has sat. From 30 days it turns into a warning triangle.
$ boxThe sell price. Tap and type; it saves on its own.
CostWhat you paid, under the price. Notes under it opens the note.
ShelfBottom right: where the phone is.
On quote S00012On that open order: not in stock and not for another order until you remove it there.

Hold a card

Select it, read its Device history, Edit device (storage, colour, carrier, grade) or Adjust cost with a reason. A changed detail shows a small !: tap it to see what changed, when and who did it.

Select several

  1. Select (right of the chips), or hold a card › Select. Tap the cards you want. Select all (N) takes everything that matches your filters.
  2. Then Move to another shelf, Sell to start a quote, Share to send a link, or Export.
  3. Clear or Done puts the normal bar back.
