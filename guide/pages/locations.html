Available shelves

Advanced › Inventory Configuration › Locations. Your Sellable Shelf is marked Available, so phones there show as in stock. Phones anywhere else (a back room, a repair bin) are kept but not for sale until you move them onto it.

Make a shelf sellable

Pick it in Locations, press Sales pool, switch Available on.

Move phones

Home › Select › Move.

New phones

Intake puts them on Your Sub-Location. Point it at the Sellable Shelf and they are for sale as soon as the lot syncs.

Locations and your plan

How many locations your plan has is on Plans. Editing locations and shelves is on Basics and up.
