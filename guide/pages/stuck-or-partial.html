The quick fix

  1. Unplug the phone.
  2. On the Intake website, remove it from the lot: on its card press ⋮ › Remove, then Remove again to confirm.
  3. Unlock the phone and plug it back in. Tap Trust (iPhone) or Allow (Android) if it asks. It is read again from the start.

You are not charged twice. The phone's check is kept with the lot, under its IMEI and serial. Removing it and plugging it back into the same lot reuses that check; only the same phone in a new lot counts again (Checks).

"No active lot" on the card

The phone was plugged in while no lot screen was open, so it was skipped. Open the lot's screen, then unplug and plug it back in.

It went into the wrong lot

Two lots open? The one you clicked into last gets the phones. Remove it from the wrong lot (⋮ › Remove), open the right lot and plug the phone in again.

Unplugged during an erase

Don't remove it: plug it back in. The lot waits 10 minutes for it (Unplugged mid-erase).

Still not reading

  • Another data cable or USB port (Cables and hubs).
  • Close Double Puff and open it again; check for an update (Updates).
  • Add the phone by hand: Manual on the lot (Device Type, Model Name, Serial Number, then Next: Grading & Photos →).
  • Then report it with the lot number, the last 4 of the IMEI and the station version.
