Share a link

From a quote or the Home selection bar: Share. The message is filled in for you and the link shows a preview with your shop's name and the devices. The buyer taps devices, can type their own price per device, and presses Submit offer. They confirm their email with a 6-digit code the first time.

Offers

Offers › Accept, Counter or Decline, even before the buyer has confirmed. Each offer shows its estimated profit; Compare lays several side by side. Go back and forth as often as you like.

If you changed a phone

A buyer with an open offer sees the new details and is asked to check and confirm again; you can't accept until they do. The agreed price never changes by itself.

Get paid

When they accept, the invoice and how to pay are emailed. With card payments on, it has a Pay now button. Without, they pay you directly and leave a payment note on the order. Check the money arrived, then Mark paid (pick how they paid; the amount is the full balance; Reference under More info). Part payment? Type the amount: the order shows what's still owed.

Pack and ship

  1. Pack Order when the box is ready. With shipping labels set up it buys and prints the label, and only then does the buyer get the "packed" email with tracking.
  2. With a bought label the carrier's first scan marks it shipped. Otherwise Mark Delivered, with carrier and tracking if you have them.
  3. The buyer checks the delivery: they scan the devices on their link, confirm, or report a problem. You get the Sold email. No answer in a few days? It confirms itself.
