Stock

  1. On Home, filter to what you want (Find phones), then Select › Select all (N), or tick the phones.
  2. Export in the selection bar.
  3. Pick Export PDF, Export CSV or Export XLSX. It says Making your CSV of 40 devices… You can keep working., then CSV ready: with the file's name. In a browser the file downloads; in the phone app the phone's own save sheet opens (Files on an iPhone, the file picker on Android) and you pick where it goes.

Reports

Analytics › open a report › Export › PDF, CSV or XLSX. The file has exactly what the report shows for the period you picked.

A very big export

That is a lot of devices for one file. Narrow the selection or export in parts.

Your own account's data

Account menu › Help & downloads › Download my data: a copy of your account and store profile.

Share instead

Share sends buyers a link to make an offer, not a file (Sell online).

Who can export

Export is in every plan; it follows your role. Greyed with a lock means your role or plan doesn't include it.

After cancelling

A cancelled shop is read-only: you can still look at and export everything (Payments and cancelling).
