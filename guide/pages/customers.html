Find one

Customers in the bottom bar. Search by name, email or phone. Bought, Sold you and Repairs narrow the list; Sort by last seen, name, most orders or most spent.

Open one

Call, Email or Edit on top; orders, spent, sold to you and repairs; contact details, addresses and every order.

On a sale

Find contact on Take Payment, type a new one, or Scan driver's license. Same phone or email as someone you have? They are reused, not doubled.

Someone you buy from

Added on the lot with their license (Add the customer). It reaches your shop in the background and shows under Customers.

Buyer accounts

Someone whose online offer you accepted gets a sign-in to see their orders and invoices. Counter customers don't.

Your plan

Customer records are on Basics and up; CRM (leads and pipeline) on Growth and up (Plans).
