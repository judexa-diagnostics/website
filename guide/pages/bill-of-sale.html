Sign

  1. On Terms & Verify, the customer reads the agreement with their phones and the total filled in.
  2. They sign on the tablet (it asks them by itself; Resend Signature Request if needed) or on your screen's pad.
  3. Complete Intake. The tablet says Thank You! and resets. The lot is locked.

Sealed

The signed copy is final: nothing on it can be changed, every page carries the seal, and its link lets anyone check a copy is the original. It is emailed as your Email Bill-of-Sale choice says, and you can download it from the lot any time.

Your own bill of sale

Intake › Settings › Documents › PDF Editor shows your bill of sale with a box wherever a detail is filled in. Want your own letterhead or wording? Use any PDF, then add boxes: customer name, phone, email, address, ID number and expiry, devices, serials/IMEIs, total, date (and time) of sale, lot number, technician, your shop's name, phone and email, and the customer signature. Drag to place, drag the corner to resize, Delete removes. Preview filled shows it with sample details. Save makes a new version: the next sale uses it; every bill already signed keeps the one it was signed on.

A template missing a box never stops a lot: Create Lot shows a yellow PDF Template warning naming what's missing.
