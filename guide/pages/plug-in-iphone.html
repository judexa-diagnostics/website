Every iPhone

  1. Open the lot's screen on the Intake website and keep it open (Create a lot).
  2. Plug the iPhone into Double Puff. Plug in as many as you like at the same time; each gets its own card.
  3. On the phone: unlock it, tap Trust, type the passcode (Trust this computer). Forgot? The lot shows Needs Trust · waiting; do it, then press I Have Trusted This Phone.
  4. Wait for ✓ Device verified. Model, storage, colour, IMEI, serial, iOS, battery, locks and parts all come off the phone, usually in under a minute. Red Queued means it's still being read.
  5. Unlock it with a face or a finger whenever you like: the station is already listening (Biometrics).
  6. Next phone? Just plug it in. Up to 16 are read at once; one slow phone never holds up another.

An iPhone at the Hello screen

If your shop chose Set it up, leave it plugged in: no taps needed. The card says Setting up this iPhone, and it reaches the home screen in about a minute (Auto activate).

The diagnostic app

When the card says Install the inphox app: on the iPhone open the App Store and search “inphox”, install it and keep the phone plugged in. The station pairs it by itself, runs the tests and the app removes itself when the result is in (The diagnostic app on iPhone).

Which phone is which?

Unplug the phone in your hand and plug it back in. Its grading window opens by itself, so you never hunt through the cards.

"No active lot" on a card

That phone was plugged in while no lot screen was open, so it was skipped. Open the lot's screen, then unplug the phone and plug it back in. A phone that was plugged in while the lot was open always finishes into that lot, even if you close the screen while it's still being read.

Won't read

Unlock it and tap Trust again, then try another cable or port. Still nothing: A phone stuck or half read, or add it by hand with Manual on the lot.
