What each card shows

IMEI · SerialThe full numbers, to match the phone in your hand (also on its label and in Settings › General › About on the phone).
Plugged inUpdates within a few seconds of plugging or unplugging. Untick Plugged in only to see phones from the last 6 hours.
Find MyOn: it can't be erased until the owner signs out. Off: fine. Not checked: run the IMEI check on the lot first.
Why not?Every card lists, in plain words, why it can't be activated or erased right now.

Activate and erase

Tick phones (or Select all), then Activate or Erase (Erase from Station phones). What happens at the Hello screen by itself is set in Auto activate.
