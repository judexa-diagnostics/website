Printing

With Auto-Print Label on Save on (My Settings) and the printer connected, the label prints as you save each phone. Printer off? It says Printer Not Connected: connect it, or Skip Printing and print from the card later (⋮ › Print Label).

Printers

A small thermal label printer with 50 × 50 mm labels, by USB or Bluetooth, or a ZPL label printer. Use Chrome or Edge: other browsers can't reach the printer. Label printing is on Growth and up.

Change the label

  1. Intake › Settings › Labels. Pick Label for (label printer or ZPL printer) and the Label size.
  2. Add from the bar: Text, Field, Barcode, QR, Logo, Line, Box. Drag to move, drag the corner to resize.
  3. Fields fill in per phone: {model} {storage} {grade} {color} {carrier} {battery} {imei} {serial} {price} {notes} {lot_id}. Text in [square brackets] is left out when its field is empty.
  4. Save label: the next label uses it. Reset to default goes back to the standard label.
