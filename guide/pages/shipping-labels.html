Where

Advanced › Integrations › Shipping Settings. Shipping integration is on Basics and up.

Set it up

  1. Make a free account with the shipping provider named on the page, with your business email, and add a card there: it charges you for each label. inphox never sees it.
  2. In the provider's settings, copy the test key. Paste it here, fill the Ship-From Address (Copy from Company) and Save. Buy one free test label to check it works.
  3. Ready for real labels: paste the live key, set the mode to live, Save.

Then

Pack Order on a paid online order buys the label and prints it. Payment never does this by itself: you press Pack when the box is ready. Without this, keep using Mark Delivered with your own carrier and tracking.
