Steps

  1. Select › Sell from Home, or Sales › New and scan them in. A phone already on another order is refused: it says which one.
  2. Check the prices. Type in a $ box and the profit and % under it change as you type. The ⚙ holds Discard order, Mark paid, Edit invoice and the name.
  3. Take Payment & Complete. Find contact for anyone you know, type a new name, phone and email, or Scan driver's license (it opens Intake and comes back with them on the order).
  4. How they paid: Cash, Card, a bank transfer or payment app, Check, or More. Then Take Payment & Complete.
  5. Sold. The bill of sale is emailed. No tax is added. Counter sales never create a login for the customer.

Confirm link (optional)

Email the customer the confirm-the-devices link and the order reads Waiting for customer until they confirm. The sale itself is already done.

The sell price

Every phone gets a suggested sell price: what you paid plus your markup (10% unless you change it), rounded up, never below what you paid.

When something is down

A check or a service that doesn't answer never stops a sale: the phone is marked check later and the order goes on. A payment that failed is never shown as paid.
