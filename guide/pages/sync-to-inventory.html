Sync to Cloud

  1. After Complete Intake you land on the lot page.
  2. ⋮ › Sync to Cloud › OK. A phone not graded yet? It asks; the ungraded ones wait until you grade them.
  3. Synced! Each phone is now in your inventory on the shelf from Your Sub-Location. The menu now reads Synced - Click Here To View.

Partial or failed

The menu says so: Retry Sync. Phones already in aren't added twice.

"Shop is busy"

Your inventory took too long to answer. Wait a moment and sync again: nothing was lost, and the customer and phones aren't added twice.

Wrong grade later

In your shop: hold the card on Home › Edit device (One phone).
