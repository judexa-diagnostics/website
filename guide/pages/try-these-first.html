"No active lot"

Nobody is on a lot's screen on the Intake website. Open the lot (New Intake, or My Lots), then unplug the phone and plug it back in.

Station says waiting

Open the lot's screen. Check Double Puff is signed in as the same person as the website (Settings › Account). Still waiting? Reload the lot screen.

Intake paused

You were away. Press Resume.

Phone won't read

Unlock it and tap Trust (or Allow), then unplug and plug it back in. Try another data cable or port (Trust this computer).

Card stuck or half filled

Unplug, remove it from the lot (⋮ › Remove), plug it back in. Not charged twice (A phone stuck or half read).

No unlock seen

Not a failure. Click the button and unlock the phone within 20 seconds.

"No price for …"

Your price list has no price for that phone and grade. Type what you'll pay.

Station acting up

Check the strip at the top of Double Puff for an update and press Download & restart. Otherwise close Double Puff and open it again.

Website acting up

Reload the page. Nothing you saved is lost.

Can't sign in

Use the same email your owner invited. Signed in from before the switch? Sign out once and press Continue again (Sign in).

Greyed with a lock

It isn't in your plan. Nothing is broken and nothing you made there is lost (Plans).
